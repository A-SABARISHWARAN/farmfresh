How It Works

Four simple steps from soil to plate

A transparent, role-based marketplace where farmers, consumers and administrators each get exactly what they need.

01

Farmers Register

Farmers create profiles, get verified by the admin, and list their harvest with live prices and stock.

02

Consumers Browse

Search and filter by category, price, availability and location. View full farmer and product details.

03

Order & Pay Securely

Add to cart or wishlist, checkout with UPI / Card / COD. Passwords hashed, inputs validated.

04

Track & Review

Follow your delivery live, rate the produce, and raise complaints — all handled from the admin dashboard.
